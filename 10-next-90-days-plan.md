# PainBridge — Next 90 Days Plan

**Document type:** Standalone execution plan
**Intended uses:** Team meetings, student task assignment, weekly tracking
**Status:** Active plan, v1 (July 2026)

---

## The one rule

The 90-day objective is **not** a product launch. It is: *one validated-feel
prototype, one clinic partner, twenty clinician reactions, thirty pilot
patients queued, one R21 aims page in a program officer's inbox.* Anything
that doesn't serve one of those five outcomes waits.

---

## Days 1–30 — Sharpen and ground

**Intellectual work**
- Draft the Pain Legibility Cascade concept paper (doubles as R21
  Significance/Innovation; source: `03-painbridge-conceptual-model.md`).
- Write the regulatory rationale memo (non-device positioning; source:
  `09-regulatory-ethical-risk-register.md` §0).
- Freeze MVP scope per `02-mvp-wedge-decision.md` §4. No additions without a
  written trade.

**Clinician discovery (target: 15–20 interviews)**
- Pain medicine, rheumatology, primary care, PT.
- Instrument: 2–3 **mock Phenotype Reports** (fake patients, made in a
  document editor — no code) + three questions: *Would you read this? What
  would you cut? Would it change your assessment?*
- This is the cheapest, highest-yield validation available. Do it before any
  app code.

**Patient discovery (target: 15–20 interviews)**
- Include ≥5 in the target second language.
- Elicit pain descriptions and idioms verbatim (consent + recording) —
  **corpus collection starts here, not with software.**

**Team/student tasks**
- Competitive teardown across the 10 categories in
  `01-core-innovation-thesis.md` §6.
- Instrument licensing/permission audit (painDETECT and CSI have usage
  terms — verify now, before building on them).
- IRB protocol drafting for the interview study.

## Days 31–60 — Prototype and partner

**Build (scope: `02-mvp-wedge-decision.md` §4; demo spec:
`04-killer-demo-script.md`)**
- Three polished mock Phenotype Reports (static HTML → PDF) — first build.
- Then the clickable MVP: 10-step flow, LBP + widespread screen, English,
  deterministic logic, PDF output. No accounts, no chat, no navigation.

**Clinical partner**
- Secure one pain clinic or academic pain center as pilot site with a named
  clinical champion. **The single highest-leverage act of the quarter.**
- Simple pilot agreement: free during pilot; letter of intent for paid
  deployment if success gates are met.

**Preliminary data**
- Run the clinician vignette study (Study 3 in
  `05-five-year-research-program.md`): 15–20 clinicians, mock reports vs.
  narrative-only — publishable, grant-citable, needs no product.

**Grants (calendar: `06-grant-roadmap.md` §9)**
- R21 specific aims page drafted.
- Identify NIH HEAL FOAs; email one program officer with the aims page.
- CTSA pilot application for the vignette/qualitative studies.

**Commercialization**
- Apply to regional I-Corps.
- Begin the customer-discovery log with clinic administrators: who buys
  intake tools, current intake cost in minutes and dollars.

## Days 61–90 — Pilot and position

**Pilot**
- IRB-approved feasibility pilot (N≈20–30) at the partner clinic: completion
  rates, time, clinician usefulness ratings, read time, patient
  acceptability/felt validation. Gates in `02-mvp-wedge-decision.md` §5.

**Corpus**
- First curated idiom set (~100 expressions, one language) with bilingual
  clinician review — the seed of research Stream B and the NIMHD/PCORI story.

**Grants**
- Full R21 draft for the next cycle.
- I-Corps interviews underway; HEAL SBIR deadlines scanned; small-business
  entity formation started if SBIR timing requires.

**Data/IP housekeeping** (from `08-data-and-ip-strategy.md` §7)
- Tiered consent finalized with IRB; BAA-ready hosting stood up;
  data-governance charter written; trademark filed; corpus schema defined.

## Explicitly not doing in these 90 days

- Additional condition pathways
- Any second language in-product
- Care navigation, dashboards, education libraries
- Mobile apps, accounts, chat interface
- Open-web/consumer launch
- Any diagnostic or probabilistic output
